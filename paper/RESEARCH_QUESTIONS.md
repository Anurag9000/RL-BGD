# Research Questions and Hypotheses

- H1: Vanilla BGD can reduce forgetting relative to Adam under short CRL streams while still learning stationary tasks.
- H2: Repeated positive-curvature evidence can reduce posterior uncertainty and late-life plasticity.
- H3: Controlled tempering maintains an uncertainty floor and improves late-life adaptation.
- H4: Surprise-driven tempering can improve retention/plasticity without boundary callbacks.
- H5: Repeated replay usage can create posterior overconfidence unless evidence accounting is corrected.
- H6: Posterior variance predicts functional parameter importance.
- H7: Previous sigma predicts subsequent parameter movement.
- H8: Recurrent context inference and Bayesian consolidation solve complementary problems.
- H9: Posterior policy sampling may improve exploration.
- H10: Any benefit should survive validation across more than one RL algorithm family.

These are hypotheses, not conclusions.
